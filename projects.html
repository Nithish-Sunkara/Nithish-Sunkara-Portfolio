Healthcare Operations & Patient Analytics

Cleaned and analyzed 20,000 synthetic patient records to examine departmental volume, length of stay, and operational performance. Built SQL analyses and a three-page Power BI dashboard showing a 15.49% readmission rate and $18.16K average billing amount in this synthetic dataset.

Business question

How do patient volumes, length of stay and readmissions vary across hospital departments?

My approach

Validated and standardized a synthetic 20,000-row dataset, queried department-level metrics in SQL, and assembled a three-page Power BI dashboard.

Selected findings

The overall average stay was approximately 3.61 days; Neurology and Oncology showed longer averages, around 5.94 and 5.89 days, respectively. These are exploratory patterns in synthetic data, not real-world clinical conclusions.

NYC Citi Bike & Weather Analytics

Developed a SQL ETL workflow for over 9 million trip records combined with weather data, and four Power BI dashboards exploring ridership, demand, station activity, and weather effects.

SQLETLPower BIAnalytics
View GitHub Repository ↗
View Project Details +

Business question

How do usage patterns, stations and weather conditions relate to demand for shared bicycles?

My approach

Integrated more than 9 million ride records with NYC weather data through a SQL ETL pipeline and developed four Power BI dashboards with KPI reporting.

Selected findings

Across the displayed January–June 2019 analysis, the overview reports 50.01K average daily trips, 14.18-minute average trip duration, 87.98% subscriber rides, and a 12.35% rain-associated difference in average daily ridership (46.40K on rainy days versus 52.93K on non-rainy days). These are descriptive comparisons, not evidence that rain alone caused the difference.

Power BI dashboard gallery

Explore the four dashboard pages. Figures and charts reflect the report’s current filters and date range.

Deliverables

SQL pipeline, quality checks, visual reports and documented transportation insights.
