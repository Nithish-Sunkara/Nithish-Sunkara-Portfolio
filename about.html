About Me

I'm a Data Analyst with a Master's degree in Information Systems from Indiana Institute of Technology and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science Engineering. I specialize in transforming complex datasets into meaningful insights that support business decisions, improve operational efficiency, and identify opportunities for growth.

My technical expertise includes Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, and IBM Cognos Analytics, along with analytical libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Scikit-learn. I work across the data analytics lifecycle, including data collection, cleaning, transformation, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

Through hands-on projects, I've analyzed 9 million+ NYC Citi Bike trip records to explore ridership patterns, station performance, and weather impacts; examined 20,000 synthetic healthcare records to evaluate hospital operations, patient outcomes, and financial performance; and developed automotive sales and service dashboards to monitor business performance and identify trends.

In addition to my portfolio projects, I contribute as a Volunteer Data Analyst with a nonprofit organization, supporting data collection, validation, and analysis activities. My background in logistics and transportation operations has further strengthened my understanding of operational workflows, performance measurement, and practical business challenges.

I also hold the IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate, an IBM certification in Generative AI for Data Analytics careers, and a Lean Six Sigma White Belt certification, reflecting my commitment to continuous learning and analytical problem-solving.

I'm passionate about connecting data with business strategy, uncovering actionable insights, and communicating complex information through clear, impactful visualizations. My goal is to deliver reliable, data-driven solutions that help organizations make informed decisions and achieve measurable results.
