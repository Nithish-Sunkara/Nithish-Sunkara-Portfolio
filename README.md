# Nithish Sunkara | Data Analyst Portfolio

A professional, responsive portfolio website showcasing my Data Analytics projects, technical skills, and experience in transforming data into actionable business insights.

**[View Live Portfolio](https://nithish-sunkara.github.io/Nithish-Sunkara-Portfolio/)**

## Portfolio Overview

This website highlights my work in healthcare analytics, transportation analytics, and automotive business intelligence.

### Featured Projects

- **Healthcare Operations & Patient Analytics:** Analysis of 20,000 synthetic patient records using Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI.
- **NYC Citi Bike & Weather Analytics:** Analysis of 9M+ bike trips and weather-related ridership patterns using SQL and Power BI.
- **Automotive Sales Analysis:** Excel-based analysis of dealership performance and profitability.
- **Automotive Sales & Service Analytics:** IBM Cognos dashboards covering sales, recalls, and customer sentiment.

## Technologies

- **Business Intelligence:** Power BI, Tableau, IBM Cognos Analytics
